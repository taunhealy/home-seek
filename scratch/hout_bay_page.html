Unfurnished two bedroom garden cottage

Situated on Valley road close to the world of birds, this cozy and quaint Nutec cottage is part of a larger property but set back from the main house. Surrounded by an established garden with faux grass, the cottage offers an open plan lounge and kitchen with glass sliding doors that lead to the garden. Both bedrooms are off the lounge and have movable cupboards. A bathroom with shower over bath is shared by both bedrooms.

The main property boasts a large heated pool which may be utilized at owners discretion and availability.

electricity is pre paid, water is included and there is fibre Wi-fi available for an extra R500. There is parking for two cars and one socialized pet will be considered. Available from the 1st of December for long term let.
